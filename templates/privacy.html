Privacy Policy

ABT Overtime Planner · Last updated 2026-06-29

ABT Overtime Planner ("the app") is a scheduling tool for planning rehearsal calls and estimating overtime. This policy explains what data it handles.

What the app stores

  • Your planner data (rehearsal blocks, notes, settings) is stored locally in your own browser (localStorage) on your device. It is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • If you set up an optional password gate, a sign-in flag is kept in a cookie for your session only.

Calendar sync (optional)

  • If you paste a calendar iCal/ICS URL (from Google, Outlook, or iCloud), the app's server fetches that URL and parses its events so matching ones can be added to your planner. It fetches only when you sync — or, if you enable auto-sync, periodically while the app is open.
  • The URL is stored only in your browser (localStorage); it is not saved on our servers. The server uses it solely to fetch and return events for your request and does not retain the calendar contents.
  • There is no Google sign-in / OAuth — the app never accesses your Google, Outlook, or iCloud account or credentials, only the public feed at the URL you provide.
  • Remove the URL in Settings to stop syncing.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your calendar data for advertising.
  • We do not store your planner or calendar contents on our servers.

Data removal

Clearing your browser's site data removes the locally stored planner and any saved iCal URL. There is no server-side account to delete.
