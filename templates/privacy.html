Privacy Policy

ABT Overtime Planner · Last updated 2026-06-29

ABT Overtime Planner ("the app") is a scheduling tool for planning rehearsal calls and estimating overtime. This policy explains what data it handles.

What the app stores

  • Your planner data (rehearsal blocks, notes, settings) is stored locally in your own browser (localStorage) on your device. It is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • If you set up an optional password gate, a sign-in flag is kept in a cookie for your session only.

Google Calendar access (optional)

  • If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the app uses Google OAuth to read your calendar events and (for two-way sync) create or update events you ask it to. It requests only calendar and basic-profile (email) scopes.
  • Your Google access token is stored in your own signed session cookie to make calendar requests on your behalf. It is not shared with anyone else, and each user only ever accesses their own calendar.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the app's Settings, which revokes the token. Google's handling of your data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your calendar data for advertising.
  • We do not store your planner or calendar contents on our servers.

Data removal

Clearing your browser's site data removes the locally stored planner. Disconnecting Google Calendar in Settings revokes the app's access to your Google account.
